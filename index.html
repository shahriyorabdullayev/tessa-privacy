Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile puzzle game Tessa handles information.

Information Tessa collects

Tessa does not require an account and does not currently collect or transmit personal information, gameplay analytics, advertising identifiers, precise location, contacts, photos, or other user content.

Game settings, progress, selected themes, scores and similar gameplay data are stored locally on your device so the game can remember your preferences and progress.

Internet, analytics and advertising

The current version of Tessa does not integrate third-party advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, cloud-storage or social-login SDKs. Tessa does not sell personal information.

Apple or Google may independently process information related to downloading, purchasing, updating or reviewing the app under their own privacy policies. That processing is controlled by the relevant app store, not by Tessa.

Local data and retention

Local game data remains on your device until it is reset or the app is removed, subject to any device or operating-system backup/restore behavior that you control.

Contacting support

If you contact support by email, we receive the email address and any information you choose to include in your message. We use that information only to respond to your request and manage the support conversation.

Children's privacy

Tessa is not designed to collect personal information from children. Because the game itself does not request accounts or personal information, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app. If you believe personal information has been sent to us through support, contact us so we can address it.

Security

We limit the app's data handling to local gameplay information and avoid unnecessary collection. No method of electronic storage or communication can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to this policy

If Tessa later adds features such as analytics, advertising, online accounts, cloud saves or other services that change how data is handled, this policy will be updated before or when those features are released. The “Last updated” date above will also change.
